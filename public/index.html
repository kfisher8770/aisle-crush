What's the deal?

It's a little game for the wedding. Every single here has a friend in their corner playing wingman.

If you're a wingman

Build a profile for your single friend and talk them up. Then catch them in the act all night, a speech, a dance, a phone number, a fifth drink, and snap a photo. Every moment you log scores them points.

If you're just here for the fun

You don't have to be single to join in. Flip through the singles, back the ones you like, and pile on the reactions. Mostly it's for the laughs.

If you're single

See someone you'd actually want to meet? Match them and send their wingman a stack of points. You can also build your own profile if nobody's done it for you.

Most points by the end of the night wins Best Wingman. Now go make some introductions.
